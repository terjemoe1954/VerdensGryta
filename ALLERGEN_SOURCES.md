# Allergen sources

Last checked: 2026-10-12

VerdensGryta uses the 14 regulated allergen categories as its profile vocabulary:

- Mattilsynet, “De 14 allergenene som alltid skal merkes”: https://www.mattilsynet.no/mat-og-drikke/merking-av-mat/slik-skal-allergenene-merkes/de-14-allergenene
- Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011, Annex II: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2011/1169/oj/eng

Recipe data distinguishes declared allergens, possible/product-dependent allergens, and unknown data. It is informational only. Users must check the current product label and consider cross-contact; the app does not claim that a dish is safe for an allergy.

Demo allergen entries are editorial examples and must be checked against the exact products used before publication.
